How it works

Four steps from a rough thought to a plan.

You do not need a technical background. The studio asks plain questions and shows its working at every step.

1

Discover

Tell the studio about a pain point, a business process, an idea or an AI system you already have.

2

Define

Review a short profile of 12 details. Each one is marked Detected, Inferred or Confirmed. Nothing is scored until you confirm.

3

Assess

Get a risk score from 0 to 100, with the reasons behind it, checked against five reference sources.

4

Act

Work through ranked actions, plan the ones you will take, and export a report to share.
